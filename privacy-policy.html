Airgap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Airgap (package com.mukassir.airgap) is an Android app that runs AI language models entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Airgap does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

No internet access

Airgap does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to the internet or to any server. All AI processing happens locally on your device.

Information we collect

None. Airgap has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The developer never receives your chats, prompts, model files, or any information about you or your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Chats and settings: your conversations, system prompts and preferences are stored in a local database on your device only.
  • Model files: GGUF model files you choose to load are read from your device storage and stay on your device.
  • Backups: Android cloud backup is disabled for Airgap, so its data is not copied to any backup service.

You can delete your data at any time from within the app or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Permissions

  • Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC): used only to let you lock the app with your fingerprint or face. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never accessible to Airgap.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 12 and below only): used only to read model files you select.

Export, import and sharing

If you choose to export chats, Airgap creates a file and hands it to Android's share sheet or file picker. Where it goes is entirely your choice; Airgap does not send it anywhere itself. Imported files are read locally.

Text-to-speech

Read-aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine. Airgap passes text only to that on-device service. If you have chosen a TTS engine from another provider, its handling is governed by that provider's policy.

Children

Airgap does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
